A portfolio rebuild starts as a visual problem and quickly becomes a maintenance problem.

The first question is what it should look like. The better question is how it will keep changing without becoming expensive to touch.

## The split

The split that matters is simple:

- content belongs in content files
- rendering belongs in components
- validation belongs near the loading boundary
- experiments belong behind small interfaces

Once that split exists, the site gets easier to change.

## The useful constraint

The site should feel authored, but the content should not be trapped in the authored surface.

That is the balance: strong visual language, low update friction. If either side wins completely, the site becomes less useful.
