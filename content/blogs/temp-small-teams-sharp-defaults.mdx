Small teams do not have enough time to rediscover their taste every week.

The best thing a small team can do is decide what kind of product it is making, and then let that decision remove work. Not remove care. Remove ambiguity.

## Defaults are product decisions

A good default is not a shortcut. It is a repeated decision that no longer needs to be argued from first principles.

That applies to:

- spacing
- copy tone
- motion
- error states
- what gets shipped now
- what gets deliberately left alone

## Velocity without blur

Moving fast is not the same thing as moving vaguely.

When the decision frame is sharp, speed feels calm. People know what belongs, what does not, and which tradeoffs are acceptable for this version.

That calm is underrated. It is one of the few advantages small teams can reliably manufacture.
